Stop by stop

    The Bishop decision

    Default: take the bypass (US-6 → 95 → 95A). Low desert, one 7,149′ pass, no snow summits.

    Route after Bishop
    Bishop charger

    Both are 250 kW, so the car charges at the same speed. Lot 13 is on the route near Schat’s. Rocking W has twice the stalls but sits past the US-6 turnoff, so Auto picks it only for the 395 route.

    Stay on 395 only if QuickMap shows no chain controls to the Nevada line and the Deadman and Conway cameras show dry road. It saves about 1½ hours. To switch, delete nav stops 3–6.
